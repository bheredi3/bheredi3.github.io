Water Conservation in Arizona

Water conservation is important to Arizona's future. With ongoing drought conditions and increasing water demands, it is important for Arizona residents to understand the importance of using water responsibly.

At Mely's Landscaping, we understand the importance of maintaining beautiful properties while being mindful of Arizona's water resources. Proper irrigation and responsible water use can help reduce unnecessary water waste while keeping your landscaping healthy.

Learn more about water conservation in Arizona by visiting the Arizona Department of Water Resources.
